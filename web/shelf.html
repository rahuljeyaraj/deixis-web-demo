The shelf

Twelve things, two rows, an arm on each side. This is the scene the end demo happens in — a look at it, nothing more.

Four of the things are identical bottles and two are identical mugs: no word tells them apart, which is the whole point. From where the person sits, two things side by side are 5.2° apart — about the width of three fingers held out at arm’s length — so pointing alone cannot settle which one they mean. The green spot on the near edge is where a thing is put into their hand, on their left.
