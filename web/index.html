Privacy
  • Your camera video never leaves your browser. The hand tracking runs here; only the fingertip’s points on the table are sent.
  • Your voice is sent once per command, turned into words in memory, then dropped. It is never saved.
  • The server writes one line per command to its log: the words and what the robot did. No audio, no video, no address.
