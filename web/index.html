deixis

This demo needs a laptop or desktop browser.

Telling a robot where. Point at a block and say put that here. deixis works out which block that means and which spot here means, and hands the arm the millimetres — no names, no coordinates.

Three things have to work for that sentence: something to see the table and find what is on it, something to work out which thing and which place were meant, and a body that can move. deixis is the middle one. We wrote each robot’s own code as well, but that part is different for every robot — deixis is the piece all three of our demos share.

In this demo the seeing is switched off. Every object and every position in this virtual world is already known, so no AI model works out what is on the table or where it is. The body is an off-the-shelf SO-101 arm.

The seeing runs for real in our other two demos — a pen plotter and a floor vacuum, each with a camera and NVIDIA Cosmos finding things in the room, and deixis still saying where.

    Use your own Nebius key

    You don’t need one. With your own key, Nemotron answers you even when today’s free commands are used up. Get a key from Nebius Token Factory.

    Your key goes to our server only to call Nebius for your own commands. It isn’t saved or logged, and it’s gone when you close this tab.

    Privacy
    • Your voice is sent once per command, turned into words in memory, then dropped. It is never saved.
    • The server writes one line per command to its log (the words, what the robot did, the tokens it used) and one per step (the guess shown, yes, done). No audio, no address.
    • To share the free AI commands fairly, the server counts today’s commands per visitor, by a random cookie and a scrambled form of your address. The counts stay in memory and are never logged.
    • If you add your own Nebius key, it is sent with each command only to call Nebius. It is never saved or logged.
